🧮 Clinical Calculators for MLS Learning

Educational estimates only; verify units and local validated reference intervals before clinical use.

How to use: Open one calculator and enter values; results update automatically as you type.
1. Dilution Calculator · 200L–500L＋

Use C₁V₁ = C₂V₂; enter any three positive values and leave exactly one blank.

2. RBC Indices Calculator · 300L–500L＋
3. WBC & RBC Reference Checker · 200L–500L＋
4. eGFR Calculator · 2021 CKD-EPI · 400L–500L＋
5. Serum Osmolality Estimator · 300L–500L＋

Enter sodium in mmol/L; glucose and urea/BUN may be mmol/L or conventional mg/dL—the supplied threshold safeguard is applied automatically.

6. Body Mass Index (BMI) · 100L–200L＋

BMI is an educational adult screening measure, not a diagnosis; adult categories are not appropriate for children or adolescents.
